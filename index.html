Calculation basis & rounding

Rating = 30 × (counted 5-star reviews / all counted reviews) − 3 × (counted 1-star + counted 2-star reviews). Three- and four-star reviews reduce the five-star share but have no separate three-point deduction. The denominator is reviews, not all lessons or distinct students.

This is an independent planning calculator using a formula reconstructed from a September 2026 ranking. It is not an official scoring system. Confirm that the 30-point base, three-point deduction, review eligibility, cutoff, and rounding rules still apply to the month you select.

The final result is rounded to two decimals, with exact halves rounded away from zero. An official result can differ by 0.01 in tie cases. Negative results are shown without an assumed zero floor. No rating is calculated when there are no counted reviews; confirm any official default separately.
